# 🌿 EcoTrack AI – Carbon Footprint Calculator

> Measure • Understand • Reduce Your Carbon Footprint

EcoTrack AI is an AI-powered web application that helps users calculate, analyze, and reduce their carbon footprint based on their daily lifestyle. The platform combines Artificial Intelligence, interactive dashboards, and personalized recommendations to promote sustainable living and environmental awareness.

---

## 📌 Overview

Climate change is one of the biggest challenges facing our planet today. Many people are unaware of how their everyday activities contribute to carbon emissions.

EcoTrack AI solves this problem by allowing users to calculate their annual carbon footprint based on:

- 🚗 Transportation
- ⚡ Electricity Consumption
- 🍽️ Food & Diet
- 🛍️ Lifestyle & Shopping Habits

The application then provides personalized suggestions to help users reduce their emissions and adopt a more sustainable lifestyle.
